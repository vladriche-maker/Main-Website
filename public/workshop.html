The problem

You wear the same four things.

Be honest. Open your closet right now.

There's the suit you bought for a wedding. The blazer that was on sale. The trousers you haven't sat down in since you bought them. And then the four things you actually wear, on rotation, every single week.

Here's the thing. Your first move is going to be buying more. Unfortunately, more is how you got here.

I know because I did it. I followed GQ for years. Bought whatever the magazine said was in. Two years later it was out, and I was back at the store. I was even a custom clothier at the time, giving other men advice while making the same mistakes myself. It took me three years to unlearn it.

You don't need three years. You need somebody to tell you which pieces are working, which ones a tailor can save, and which ones need to go. Then you need one outfit built from what's left.

That's this workshop.

The short version

Bring your closet. In 90 minutes we sort everything into keep, tailor, or burn, and build one outfit from what you already own. Seventeen dollars.

How the 90 minutes run

Measured out like a fitting.

00 – 25 min

The Fit Test

Pull three pieces and put them on. We check shoulders, jacket length, sleeves, trouser rise, and the Dreaded Triangle. Fit is comfort. If it's tight, you can't be well-dressed. No middle ground.

You leave with: a fit score for every piece you tried.

25 – 55 min

Keep, Tailor, or Burn

What a good tailor can fix, what nobody can fix, and what was a trend the day you bought it. Skinny lapels, short jackets, hybrid shoes. We go piece by piece.

You leave with: your closet sorted and a tailor list.

55 – 80 min

The Ground-Up Build

Shoes first. Then trousers, jacket, shirt, tie. We build one complete outfit from what survived, and write down exactly what's missing.

You leave with: one outfit and a short gap list.

80 – 90 min

Your Questions

Hold up the piece you're not sure about. I'll tell you straight. Not rude. Just honest.

You leave with: no more guessing on that one jacket.
